The photo is personal. The guesswork is expensive.

Custom pet products sell on emotion. If shoppers cannot picture the finished piece, they hesitate — or they buy, then write in when the result is not what they imagined.

They cannot see the finished product

A phone photo and a blank mug or blanket do not tell the same story. Many shoppers leave before they feel sure.

“This doesn’t look like my pet”

Mismatched expectations create refunds, remakes, and long support threads after the order is already in motion.

Manual mockups do not scale

Sending proofs by hand works until holiday volume hits. A product-page preview can do that work in a few seconds.

How it works

Three steps on your product page. You keep selling through Shopify; billing for the app will run through the Shopify App Store.

STEP 1

Shopper uploads a pet photo

On your product page, they add the photo they already have — a phone snapshot is enough to start the preview.

STEP 2

They see it on the product

In a few seconds, they get a realistic preview of that pet on your portrait, mug, blanket, tee, or other POD item.

STEP 3

They add to cart with confidence

The preview is what they expected to receive. That means fewer “can you show me a mockup?” messages and fewer surprise refunds.

Questions

Is the app in the Shopify App Store yet?

Not yet. This page is for early trial interest while we validate demand. Submitting the form does not install anything on your store.

How will I be charged?

When the app is available, billing will run through the Shopify App Store. There is no separate card to enter on this page, and this form is not a charge.

What does founding pricing mean?

The first 20 stores to join the early trial will get a founding rate, to be confirmed in the $19–$49 per month range. We are not locking a final price or a launch date on this page.

Do I need to get on a video call?

No. Just send the form. If the trial is a fit, we will follow up by email.

Which products is this for?

Custom pet portraits and print-on-demand gifts — including mugs, blankets, pillows, T-shirts, and similar items sold on Shopify.

Are the photos on this page real live previews?

No. They are labeled illustrations, made to show the kind of before-and-after a shopper would see. Live quality will be proven in the trial, not claimed here.
